1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how Used-In ("the App") handles data when you install and use it within your Atlassian Confluence instance. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding our data processing practices.

2. Architecture and Data Storage
The App is built using the Atlassian Forge platform. As a cloud-hosted extension running entirely within the secure Atlassian infrastructure:

No External Servers: We do not host, store, or transmit your personal data or Confluence content to any external third-party servers managed by us.

Atlassian Infrastructure: All data processing and application storage (such as settings, application states, or user parameters) occur strictly within Atlassian’s cloud environment via Forge APIs and Forge Storage APIs.

3. Data We Collect and Process
Depending on the functionality of the App, the following information may be processed directly within your Atlassian Cloud site:

Account Identifiers: Atlassian User IDs (AccountId) to associate app configurations or actions with a specific user.

Technical Logs: Diagnostic logs generated during application runtime (processed exclusively through Atlassian Cloud monitoring tools).

Workspace Content: Data or metadata from your Confluence pages strictly necessary for the operation of the App's features.

4. How We Use Data
We use the processed data solely to:

Provide, maintain, and execute the functionalities of the App within your Confluence instance.

Save user preferences or configuration settings specific to the App.

Diagnose technical issues and improve performance.

5. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, rent, share, or trade any user data or Confluence content with third parties. All data remains within the security boundary of the Atlassian Cloud platform.

6. Data Retention and Deletion
Retention: Data stored by the App remains in your Atlassian workspace storage for as long as the App is installed.

Deletion: Uninstalling the App removes access to its stored data. Any persistent app data retained in Forge Storage is handled in accordance with Atlassian’s standard data retention and lifecycle policies.

7. Security
Because the App operates natively on the Atlassian Forge framework, it leverages Atlassian’s enterprise-grade security controls, encryption protocols (in transit and at rest), and compliance standards.

8. Your Rights
Under GDPR and other privacy laws, users have rights regarding their personal data (including access, correction, and deletion). Since all data is managed within your Atlassian Cloud tenant, administrators can manage user data directly through Atlassian Account controls.
